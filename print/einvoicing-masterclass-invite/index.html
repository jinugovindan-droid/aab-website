A complimentary masterclass on UAE e-Invoicing.

From 1 January 2027, the UAE's largest businesses must issue invoices that report themselves to the Federal Tax Authority — in near real time — with the rest of the market following through 2027. It applies to B2B and B2G transactions (B2C issuing is optional for now — but receiving still applies), and many founders haven't heard of it yet. Here is your chance to put your community a step ahead of the mandate.
